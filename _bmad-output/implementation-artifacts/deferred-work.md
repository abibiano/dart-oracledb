A caller that iterates a million rows and closes nothing therefore accumulates a million registered handles plus the O(n) `_openLobs` sweeps every concurrency guard performs, with no warning threshold and no diagnostic pointing at the close obligation. Blast radius is bounded (an open fetched handle already blocks `execute()`, so the growth is confined to the iteration) and it is entirely caller-driven, hence a follow-up. Fix: a one-shot `_log.warning` once `_openLobs` passes a threshold, naming the per-handle close obligation. target: Epic 12 follow-up (API ergonomics / diagnostics).
